Client Install: NONE. 
	Notes for Client Playing the mod. Weapons can only be accessed
	by Selecting "Next Weapon" or "Previous Weapon". There are no
	numbers assigned to the new weapons. 

Server Install: 
	1. Create a directory named Skynet under the Tribes main directory.
	2. Unzip the scripts.vol file into there.
	 
When installed The Files Should be here: 
 Tribes\skynet\scripts.vol
 Tribes\skynet\Readme.txt


To start the server with this mod go to DOS and the tribes directory and type
  tribes -mod skynet

or for a dedicated server type

   tribes -mod skynet 	-dedicated

Current Additions:
---------------------------------------------------------------
Starseige Tribes: Skynet Mod 

New Weapons- 
Lightning Rod-
Takes the place of the Elf gun. Exactly the same except it actually does a resonable ammount of damage.

Rocket Launcher-
Medium Armor only. Shoots a high powered rocket that is capable of knocking its target into oblivion.

Sniper Rifle- 
Light Armor only. A silent, high velocity projectile gun used for assassination purposes. And you thought the laser rifle was good. 

Shockwave Cannon-
Heavy Armor only, must have energy pack. This weapon fires a high powered blast that creates a huge shockwave. While not actualy damaging its target, it is capable of knocking them great distances. 

Railgun-
Medium Armor only. A cross between a chaingun and a sniper rifle. the only difference is that it actually shoots straight, while maintaing a high rate of fire. 

Gauss Gun-
Medium Armor only. An energy weapon that launches high speed fireballs. 


New Deployables 
Deployable Command Center-
Heavy Armor only. Just like the ones found in most bases. Thanks to LabRat for the origional idea. 

Laser Turret-
Light Armor only. Forget sniping! Just pop one of these down and laugh as your enemy gets assassinated before they even know what hits them.

Rocket Turret-
Medium and Heavy only. A portable version of the mother of all turrets. 

Plasma Turret-
Heavy Armor only. You know that huge thing that always kills you at the enemy base? yep it has shields too.

Force Shield-
Protect your loved ones and personal belongings with this beautiful blue wall of force. Stop those intruders in their tracks as they attempt to get to your flag. Thanks to Savage1 for the origional idea. 


New Kits- 
Flag Decoy Mine-
Someone mad you took their flag? Thow one of these behind you to cover your tracks and listen to the screams. Great fun at parties. Find and Retrive missions have a whole new meaning now. Its soo funny running into a room with 20 flags watching people trying to decide which one to pick up. :) (takes the place of grenades) 

Speed Boost-
Need that extra lift while running away? Can't make it up to the roof in that heavy armor? just inject one of these while running or jumping and you will soon be flying. (takes the place of beacons)
