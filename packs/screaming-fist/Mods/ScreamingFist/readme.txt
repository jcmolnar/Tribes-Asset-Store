--- Installation & Setup ---

1.1 - Getting Screaming Fist

Screaming Fist can not be downloaded. It will be distributed to closed beta testers via IRC DCC, ICQ transfers, or other direct means of file transfer, but not publically via a download site. If you are reading this, you already have SF, but if there seems to be a problem with your setup file, contact Ratorsniki in IRC #screamingfist, or email ratorasniki@tribalwar.com.

1.2 - Installing Screaming Fist

The installation for SF is very streamlined, and you should not have any troubles. If for some reason you do have troubles, contact Ratorsniki in IRC #screamingfist, or email ratorasniki@tribalwar.com.

1.3 - Setting up Screaming Fist

This part should be as easy as customizing Tribes.  The recommended settings are:

Detpack - P
Hand Grenade - G
Conc. Grenade - M
Rem. Mine - N
Prox. Mine - B
Remote Detonator - 9


--- Game Basics ---

2.1 - Classes

There are four classes of players in Screaming Fist:

INFANTRY

The infantry is considered standard, and will make up the majority of the unit. Equipped with light SV-198 Kevlar Body Armor, the infantry is the most versatile and the most maneuverable designation. Infantry is responsible for the front line of an given assault run, both providing cover fire for any crucial units (such as the Technician) or non-hostiles, as well as the creation of an effective "kill-zone". Standard issue for the Infantry includes the standard's load-out, as well as the Pryor-Kinson MAW (Multi-Armament Weapon), which is a M209 Assault Rifle, with a over-the-top grenade launcher and Incendiary Shell Launching receiver built in. As an additional option, the Heavy may also carry a RS345 Fromman Rocket Launcher (which is able to be used Free-Fire or Fly-by-Wire)

HEAVY INFANTRY

A more heavily armored and well equipped version of the standard Infantry unit. This specialized version of Infantry is responsible for mainly the laying down of suppressing and cover fire, at a more intense rate than possible with standard infantry. It is also designed to be an excellent "front-line" assault unit, but is limited to certain scenarios because of its speed. Because of its additional weight and encumbering nature, this unit (outfitted with heavy SV-266 Kevlar Body Armor) often requires additional units as cover. Standard weapons for the Heavy include the Heckler & Koch VP-656A 35mm Handgun, a M203 Assault Rifle w/12 gauge adapter, and a pick of a variety of supplemental packs and ordinance (including concussion grenades, pineapple grenades, and remote detonator packs).

SPECIAL OPS

The Special Ops unit designation is equipped in light Body Armor for optimal movement and sight capabilities. Along with several of the standard Infantry weapons, the Special Ops units carry a Disguise Pack, which can allow them to blend in with opposing units under optimal conditions, and a silenced XT100074 Sniper Rifle. The Special Ops unit's main purpose is infiltration, reconnaissance, and long-range battlefield support.

TECHNICIAN

The Technician unit designation is perhaps the most important and complex of all SF units. They can be equipped with light armor, and have a variety of special gear including the Console Jack (allowing them to tap into enemy remote defense units), a GROC gun, and a Deck which provides an input interface with enemy technology. Technical unit designations are mainly used for battlefield support and infiltration of enemy strongholds.
 

2.2 - Weapons

- Guns -

The Screaming Fist world is populated with many-a-way to mutilate the enemy...

Handgun; Although usually considered a sidearm, can be used effectively in most situations.

Silenced Handgun; Same as above, but almost silent.

Assault Rifle; This is your workhorse, treat it as such. Most of the time you will carry this standard issue assault rifle.

Silenced Assault Rifle; Same as above, silent but deadly.

Shotgun; This "Tactical" shotgun is used for turning close range enemies into long-ranged-dead enemies post haste. Its kickback is a small price to pay..

MAW (Multi Assault Weapon); Three modes, Assault Rifle which is basically the exact same as the standard issue assault rifle, Incendary Cannon, which fires a explosive hunk of..erm..explosives, which on contact explodes into tiny shards of explosives, which in turn explode, and the Grenade Launcher, which is a pretty standard pinapple popping 'nade hucker.

Sniper Rifle; 3 words, Head Shots Kill. Beware the silent death from afar =)

Minigun; Rotating barrels of pain. My personal favourite, theres nothing like dishing out the hurt with this baby.

Rocket Launcher; 2 modes, Standard and FBW (Fly By Wire). FBW mode launchers a guided (by you) rocket that you can steer and aim remotely directly up your enemies wazoo. 

- Gear -

GRoC (Guided Rocket Countermeasures); A Deck addition, it homes in on the energy source of FBW rockets and flies at them, destroying them on contact. Will do no damage to anything else, and requires a lock before firing.

Console Jack - A Deck addition, allows you to hack into enemy equipment, doing one of three things, making it accesible, disabling it, or destroying it, depending on your luck.

Remote Detonator - Remote detonates your remote mines from anywhere on the map. =D

- Explosives -

Handgrenade - Standard explosive grenade, throw and watch blow up.

Concussion Grenade - Throw and watch the enemy cry as it blows up and causes the blood to rush to their eyes. If ever there was a distraction...

Proximity Mine - Lay and dont run into, stick them around the enemy base for fun.

Remote Mine - For even further fun, lay these around the enemy base and wait for them to run out, then boom, you remote detonate as they run by from your hiding spot afar. >:)

Detpack - Timed explosive device that can be set (via the Tab menu, under SF Options) to explode anywhere from 5 to 99 seconds from the point you lay it at. Comes with 3 presets, 10, 20, and 30 seconds, along with the custom setting.

2.3 - Packs

Equipment for you to use..

Ammo Pack - Holds ammo, duh :P

Repair Pack - Repairs base items, turrets, teammates,...basically anything that gets hurt

Minigun Ammo Pack - A special kind of ammo pack designed to carry the extreme weight of the minigun shells. Must be bought to use the Minigun.

Disguise Pack - Does one of three things. Disguise yourself as the enemy, Disguise yourself as yourself (but why, really..heh), or Feign Death. You choose what you want it to do via the Tab menu, under SF Options.

Deck - The Techs ultimate revenge (of the nerd). Its basically starting at a sensor jammer and working its way up the destructive food chain via a few upgrades, namely the Console Jack, and the GRoC. The deck MUST be bought to operate the Jack and/or the GRoC.

Ammo Loader - Ammo is a commodity, and without it your turrets cease to function. Buy this pack and load up your turrets (and deployable stations) with ammo, so they can keep kickin' da arse.

Bug Pack - The tool of the spy. Spec Ops players can fire off a bug, and fly it remotely into the location they want to deploy, and then *fire* to deploy the bug on a wall, floor, or roof, anywhere to spy on the enemy.

2.4 - Deployables

Stuff you can use to maim, kill, or resupply

Inventory Station - Buy gear, or sell gear. Keep it filled with ammo, or it'll be useless.

Ammo Station - Walk in, get ammo, go kill stuff.

Medic Station - Since none of the other remote stations actually heal you (that would be some pretty spectacular ammo, eh?) this station was invented to keep you feeling good so you can kill stuff :)

Motion Sensor - Detects moving things...

Pulse Sensor - Detects anything not being jammed...

Camera - Detects anything in its Field of View...

Turret - Basically a little automated machine gun. Keep it full of ammo or it'll be killed easilly.

2.5 - Turrets

30MM Cannon - Big, Mean, Nasty, Fun to control. Must be manned, but will cut down stuff real quick-like.

Howitzer - Fires great big explosive shells, real far-like. Must be manned. Can be used two ways, regular explosive shells, or mine deploying shells. Mine deploying shells will fly over head dropping mines untill they hit the ground and blow up. Firing off five or six of these will result in a nasty minefeild.

Indoor Turrets - These are deployable turrets on crack, spitting out machinegun bullets like..they were machineguns :) Cannot be manned.

Rocket Turret - Cannot be manned. Fires volleys of heat-seaking rockets, will usually only track vehicles.

2.6 - Vehicles

Attack Comanche - Fast, agile, and it even has a mounted minigun. It looks pretty cool too :D

Osprey Transport - This transport is not really a helicoptor, more of a rotorcraft. It has VTOL (Vertical TakeOff and Landing), but while in flight, its engines will rotate around in the direction it is flying. Transports 4 troops to the feild, and is heavilly armored, but slow, and extremely slow turning.

--- Troubleshooting ---

4.1 - Any graphical bug/problem

If you are using Software Mode, it will cause things like terrain morphing, black textures, random crashes, etc. Screaming Fist is a hardware only game, and if you dont have a 3D card, its been time to upgrade now for a loooooooong while. May i suggest a Voodoo3 or a GeForce?

Also OpenGL as aposed to Glide will result in black textures, funky looking terrain morphing, and that sort of weirdness you can only get with a TNT ;D

My recommendation is playing SF with a voodoo 3 but then i guess we cant all... :\

4.2 - Osprey Transport

If you are getting stuck inside the Osprey, this is a known bug and hopefully will be fixed soon. In the meantime if this happens to you, we suggest you do not ride in the Osprey for the time being..

4.3 - Interface

If you are having trouble binding keys in the interface, we suggest you make your own config and execute it from the SF autoexec file located at C:\Tribes\Config\sf\autoexec.cs

This is a known problem with some computers.
